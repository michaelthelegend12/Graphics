# Graphics

Motion graphics experiments, built as plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript so they run in any browser with no build step.

## Pieces

- [`logo-intro/`](logo-intro/index.html): animated logo and title intro. Open `logo-intro/index.html` in a browser and use the Replay button to run it again.
- [`mascot/`](mascot/index.html): Pip, an original mascot. It wakes up, waves, hops twice and celebrates with confetti, then dozes off in an 8 second loop.
- [`card-payment/`](card-payment/): 15 second isometric explainer of how a card payment works (tap, terminal, bank check, approved), with smooth camera moves and sound. Watch [`card-payment.mp4`](card-payment/card-payment.mp4), or open `card-payment/index.html` for the live version.
- [`app-promo/`](app-promo/): 20 second Keynote-style promo for Lumen, a made-up banking app. A phone floats in 3D while the screens slide inside it, the balance counts up and a card flips over, with music. Watch [`app-promo.mp4`](app-promo/app-promo.mp4), or open `app-promo/index.html` for a silent live loop.
- [`smartphone-data/`](smartphone-data/): 25 second animated data story of worldwide smartphone users from 2010 to 2025. A neon line graph draws itself while the camera follows its tip, with milestone callouts at each billion, on a dark theme. Watch [`smartphone-data.mp4`](smartphone-data/smartphone-data.mp4) (1080p), or run `smartphone-data/render.py` (Python, matplotlib and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`bees-collage/`](bees-collage/): 20 second paper cutout / collage explainer on why bees matter for our food. Textured paper, layered shadows and a 12 fps stop-motion feel, with gentle music. Watch [`bees-collage.mp4`](bees-collage/bees-collage.mp4) (720p).
- [`flight-globe/`](flight-globe/): 15 second 1080p travel-vlog flight route, London to Dubai to Tokyo. A dotted 3D globe rotates while two glowing arcs draw themselves, a plane follows each arc, and city labels pop in, with a progress bar and distance counter, over an original chill lo-fi track. Watch [`flight-globe.mp4`](flight-globe/flight-globe.mp4), or run `flight-globe/render.py` (Python, numpy, OpenCV, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render the picture and `flight-globe/music.py` (numpy and scipy) to regenerate the music.
- [`vaccine-whiteboard/`](vaccine-whiteboard/): 30 second hand-drawn whiteboard explainer of how vaccines train your immune system. Lines and handwritten labels draw on stroke by stroke with a marker, simple cell characters act out the story, and an eraser wipes between scenes. Watch [`vaccine-whiteboard.mp4`](vaccine-whiteboard/vaccine-whiteboard.mp4) (720p, silent), or run `vaccine-whiteboard/render.py` (Python, numpy, OpenCV and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`night-drive/`](night-drive/): 10 second seamless-loop synthwave title card for a playlist called Night Drive. A moving neon grid floor, a big sun with scanlines and chrome lettering with a shine sweeping over it, with a looping synth pad. Watch [`night-drive.mp4`](night-drive/night-drive.mp4) (1080p), or run `night-drive/render.py` (Python, numpy, OpenCV, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`grindhouse-ad/`](grindhouse-ad/): 15 second vertical (1080x1920) ad for Grindhouse, a made-up coffee subscription. A hook in the first second, a bag, cup and beans built from plain shapes, a $14/month price callout and a call to action, cut to a 128 BPM beat with music. Watch [`grindhouse-ad.mp4`](grindhouse-ad/grindhouse-ad.mp4), or run `grindhouse-ad/music.py` (numpy and scipy) then `grindhouse-ad/render.py` (Python, numpy, OpenCV, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`jet-engine/`](jet-engine/): 20 second 1080p blueprint explainer of how a jet engine works: suck, squeeze, bang, blow. A cutaway engine with air particles flowing through it, each stage highlighted and labelled in turn, over an engine hum. Watch [`jet-engine.mp4`](jet-engine/jet-engine.mp4), or run `jet-engine/hum.py` (numpy) then `jet-engine/render.py` (Python, numpy, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`football-stats/`](football-stats/): pack of three 5 second 1080p football (soccer) broadcast stats cards in a Premier League TV graphics style. Each has a player name, a photo placeholder, a club crest placeholder and three stat bars that fill up while the numbers count, with team colour accents and slanted wipes in and out. Clubs and players are made up. Watch [`card-1-delaney.mp4`](football-stats/card-1-delaney.mp4), [`card-2-arriaga.mp4`](football-stats/card-2-arriaga.mp4), [`card-3-brandt.mp4`](football-stats/card-3-brandt.mp4) or all three joined in [`football-stats-pack.mp4`](football-stats/football-stats-pack.mp4), or run `football-stats/render.py` (Python, numpy, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render them.
- [`hurricane/`](hurricane/): 30 second 1080p documentary-style explainer of how a hurricane forms, drawn like a cutaway graphic. A pseudo-3D slab of warm ocean and atmosphere is cut in half: warm water feeds rising moist air, storm clouds tower, the spin and sinking air open the eye, and arrows trace the inflow, outflow and the winds circling the eyewall, with a satellite-view inset and ambient wind, sea and thunder sound. Watch [`hurricane.mp4`](hurricane/hurricane.mp4), or run `hurricane/sound.py` (numpy and scipy) then `hurricane/render.py` (Python, numpy, OpenCV, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
- [`avocado-toast/`](avocado-toast/): 15 second vertical (1080x1920) recipe motion graphic, avocado toast in 4 steps. Flat top-down illustrations: bread slides in and toasts, an avocado is halved, its pit pops out and it gets mashed with a fork, the spread goes on with a knife, then egg, chili flakes, pepper, basil and lemon land on top. Bouncing step numbers, colour wipes between steps and an upbeat 128 BPM track with matching sound effects. Watch [`avocado-toast.mp4`](avocado-toast/avocado-toast.mp4), or run `avocado-toast/music.py` (numpy and scipy) then `avocado-toast/render.py` (Python, numpy, Pillow and ffmpeg) to re-render it.
